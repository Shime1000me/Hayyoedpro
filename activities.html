Student Life

Clubs & Leadership Programs

At Hayole Education Project, education extends beyond the classroom. Our diverse range of student clubs and leadership programs helps students discover their passions, develop essential life skills, and build lasting friendships.

  • Moot Court — State champions and national finalists in legal debate and argumentation.
  • STEM Innovation Hub — Robotics, 3D design, electronics, and project-based learning.
  • Cultural & Arts Clubs — Music, dance, theater, and visual arts celebrating Sidama's rich heritage.
  • Sports & Athletics — Football, basketball, athletics, and competitive team sports.
  • Leadership & Debate — Student government, public speaking, and ethical leadership training.
  • Community Service — Volunteering, outreach programs, and social responsibility initiatives.
