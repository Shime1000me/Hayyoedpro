Since 2021

Founded in Vision. Driven by Commitment. Known for Excellence

Established in 2021 through a landmark collaboration between the Sidama Regional Government and the local community, Hayole Education Project is dedicated to nurturing the region's highest-achieving minds. As one of seven premier state-led boarding institutions, we deliver a transformative academic environment designed to cultivate future scientists, engineers, and national leaders.

Located in Hawassa City, Sidama Region, our school offers a high-rigor, immersion-based setting where elite scholars transition from competitive Grade 8 entry to university excellence.

Why Hayole Education Project

Advanced Academic Rigor, Holistic Growth

#1 Nationally in 2025

Home to the nation's top performers on the 2025 ESSLCE (592/600) and (590/600), achieving a 100% pass rate with all graduates scoring above 480.

STEM Beyond the Standard

University-level coursework in Calculus II, Calculus III., Linear Algebra, and Physics, paired with an AI-driven IT program developed alongside Kelem Meda Technologies.

3.2% Acceptance Rate

One of Ethiopia's most selective boarding schools, admitting only the strongest performers on the regional entrance examination.

15+ Student Clubs & Leadership Programs

From state-champion Moot Court teams and STEM innovation hubs to cultural arts, students explore their passions across a diverse range of extracurricular clubs.
