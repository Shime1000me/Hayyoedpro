Recognitions

Awards & distinctions

  • Historic National Exam Achievements: Home to Ethiopia's #1 top scorer Greaterthan Abebe Salfako (592/600) and #2 Biniam Birhan Bitala (590/600), alongside the #2 female scorer nationally (580/600) in 2025.
  • Government Recognition: Honored by government officials as one of the elite few schools nationwide where 100% of candidates passed the 2025 University Entrance Examination.
  • Flawless Graduation Rate: Maintained a perfect 100% success record in the national examination.
  • National Moot Court Excellence: Secured 1st and 2nd place nationwide at the 2024 National Moot Court Competition.
  • Regional Championship Leaders: Named overall champion of the 2023 Sidama Regional Educational Festival, winning 7 gold medals competing against 50+ elite schools.
  • Consistent State-Level Dominance: Selected 16 students to represent the academy at the Sidama Regional Festival's state-level academic competition for 3 consecutive years.
  • Multi-Disciplinary Gold Medals: Earned Gold medals across Robotics, Dance, and Music at the 2024 Regional Festival.
  • STEM & Academic Quiz Victories: Secured Gold in STEM, Athletics, and Academic Quiz & Answer competitions in 2024.
